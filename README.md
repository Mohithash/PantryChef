# Pantry Chef

**Cook from what you have.** A bring‑your‑own‑key AI kitchen assistant.

- 📷 **Scan your fridge** — photo → catalogued ingredients with quantities and categories.
- 🍳 **Suggest recipes** — 4 ideas that use your pantry, marked with what you have vs. what to buy; respects diet, allergies, servings and max cooking time.
- 🔁 **Swap?** — instant substitution advice for any missing ingredient.
- 👨‍🍳 **Cook mode** — one big step at a time with a wavy progress bar.
- ❤️ **My recipes** — save favourites, track how often you cooked them.
- 🛒 **Grocery list** — missing ingredients land here in one tap.

BYOK: works with Claude (Anthropic) or any OpenAI‑compatible endpoint. Your key never leaves your device except to your chosen provider. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

Built with Jetpack Compose + Material 3 Expressive, Room, AGP 9.4. `./gradlew :app:assembleRelease`
